Huawei Rate-Limiting Technical Standards (NE8000 vs S6730):

Huawei NE8000 Router: Uses interface QoS CAR rate-limiting (qos car cir <cir> cbs <cbs>). Standard CIR is in Kbps and CBS is in Bytes with a multiplier of 125 (1.0s buffer).
Huawei S6730 Switch: Uses MQC (Modular QoS) architecture (traffic classifier + traffic behavior + traffic policy). Color-blind mode with CIR=PIR and CBS=PBS with a multiplier of 150 (cir × 150) for line-rate hardware policing on campus/aggregation switches.
